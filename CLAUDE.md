# Rules for anyone editing this site (people and AI sessions)

1. **Always `git pull` before editing `index.html`, and again right before you commit.**
   More than one session edits this file. Editing an old copy and pushing it deletes other people's work.
2. **Never remove the `#/sell` page** (`function pgSell` and the route `sell:pgSell` in `var map={...}`).
   It is the shop landing page linked in every StylePlay invitation (Instagram DMs, emails, posts).
   The menu item "Sell with us" also points to it. If it disappears, every invitation link breaks.
   An hourly check in StylePlay AI HQ alerts the founder when it is missing.
3. StylePlay accepts every fashion category used to build a look: clothing, shoes, bags, hats, jewelry and accessories. Do not write "clothing shops only".
